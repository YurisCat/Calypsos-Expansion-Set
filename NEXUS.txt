[center][size=6][color=#B78A50][b]Calypso's Expansion Set[/b][/color][/size][/center]

[center]14 new idols with unusual carrying challenges, sticky-note symbols and short Chinese hints for custom and supported vanilla idols.[/center]


[size=4][color=#B78A50][b]The Idols[/b][/color][/size]

[b][color=#B78A50]01 ·[/color] Jumping[/b]
Repeats normal-strength jumps with random jump sounds.

[b][color=#B78A50]02 ·[/color] Close-Up[/b]
Smoothly zooms in when picked up and restores your view when put down.

[b][color=#B78A50]03 ·[/color] Voice-Controlled[/b]
[b]Microphone volume drives forward movement[/b], replacing keyboard/gamepad directions; turning and other actions remain available. Shared eyes: red when idle, blue when sound permits movement.

[b][color=#B78A50]04 ·[/color] Upside-Down[/b]
[b]Flips the view[/b], reverses both look axes and left/right movement; forward/backward movement, HUD and menus stay normal.

[b][color=#B78A50]05 ·[/color] Duck-Step[/b]
Random duck calls accompany your footsteps.

[b][color=#B78A50]06 ·[/color] Extreme-Speed[/b]
Continuous horizontal movement at [b]5× normal walking speed[/b], even while crouching, jumping or in menus. Steer on the ground; direction locks in the air. No sprint boost; put it down to stop.

[b][color=#B78A50]07 ·[/color] Delayed-Movement[/b]
Movement changes and releases arrive [b]0.3 seconds late[/b]; looking and interacting stay immediate.

[b][color=#B78A50]08 ·[/color] Relay[/b]
Carrying builds fatigue and slows ground/air movement; [b]passing to someone else resets it[/b]. Putting it down only pauses fatigue. Rules follow the living-player count, with gentler solo rules; shared eyes fade from green to red.

[b][color=#B78A50]09 ·[/color] Suspicious[/b]
Random monster sounds come [b]from the holder[/b] and can draw hearing-capable monsters to them.

[b][color=#B78A50]10 ·[/color] Wide-Body[/b]
Wider, shorter body with a lower, stretched view; [b]collision size stays normal[/b].

[b][color=#B78A50]11 ·[/color] Monster-Disguise[/b]
Each pickup chooses a monster appearance from this run's spawned or pending/respawning types, falling back to the weeping doll. Hides the held idol and flashlight while carried; grants no monster powers or targeting changes.

[b][color=#B78A50]12 ·[/color] Pixel[/b]
Pixelates the world and HUD.

[b][color=#B78A50]13 ·[/color] Outline[/b]
White outlines on black for the world and HUD; [b]no seeing through walls[/b].

[b][color=#B78A50]14 ·[/color] Big-Head[/b]
Others see your enlarged head; you get a strong fisheye view of the world and HUD.

[size=4][color=#B78A50][b]Options & Notes[/b][/color][/size]

Edit [b]BepInEx/config/calypso.invokyr.expansionset.cfg[/b] after first launch; restart after changes.

[b]Default settings[/b]
[spoiler]
[b]NaturalSpawning = true[/b]
New idols enter natural spawn pools.

[b]DisableBoardPawnCollision = true[/b]
Walk through board progress pawns to move and kick dice more easily. Visuals, animations, board, dice and event pieces stay normal. Local setting; matching settings recommended.

[b]ShowIndividualIdolNames = false[/b]
Enable individual Chinese names; otherwise keep vanilla names and use one shared MOD name. Hover hints remain enabled.

[b]DebugMode = false[/b]
Optional test tools/logs. Enable it for the F8 window; only the host can spawn idols. Normal play needs no debug mode.
[/spoiler]

[i]In-game names and hints are currently Chinese.[/i]

[i]Development release: full multiplayer/save compatibility remains unverified; relay fatigue and disguise history are not saved.[/i]

Detailed setup, testing and limitations are on GitHub below.

[size=4][color=#B78A50][b]雕像一览[/b][/color][/size]

[b][color=#B78A50]01 ·[/color] 弹跳[/b]
按原版力度自动连续跳跃，随机播放起跳音效。

[b][color=#B78A50]02 ·[/color] 近景[/b]
拿起平滑放大视野，放下平滑恢复。

[b][color=#B78A50]03 ·[/color] 声控[/b]
[b]麦克风音量驱动前进[/b]，替代键盘／手柄方向输入，仍可转向和执行其他动作；待机红眼、声音允许前进时蓝眼，全员可见。

[b][color=#B78A50]04 ·[/color] 倒立[/b]
[b]画面倒立[/b]，视角双轴及左右移动反向；前后移动、HUD 与菜单正常。

[b][color=#B78A50]05 ·[/color] 鸭步[/b]
走路时跟随脚步随机鸭叫。

[b][color=#B78A50]06 ·[/color] 极速[/b]
持续以[b]正常步行的五倍水平速度[/b]移动，蹲下、跳跃和菜单中也不停；地面可换向，空中锁方向，奔跑不加速，放下停止。

[b][color=#B78A50]07 ·[/color] 迟钝[/b]
移动方向与松键[b]延迟 0.3 秒[/b]，视角和交互即时。

[b][color=#B78A50]08 ·[/color] 接力[/b]
累计持有越久，地面与空中移动越慢；[b]换人清空疲劳[/b]，放下仅暂停。规则随存活人数切换，单人较宽松；眼睛随疲劳由绿渐红，全员可见。

[b][color=#B78A50]09 ·[/color] 疑神[/b]
[b]持有者身上[/b]随机传来怪物声音，可能引来有听觉的怪物。

[b][color=#B78A50]10 ·[/color] 宽体[/b]
身体变宽变矮，视点降低、画面横向拉伸，[b]实际碰撞体积不变[/b]。

[b][color=#B78A50]11 ·[/color] 怪相[/b]
每次拿起随机变为本局已生成、待生成或待重生的怪物外观，空池使用哭泣人偶；持有时隐藏雕像与手电光，不获得怪物能力或改变仇恨。

[b][color=#B78A50]12 ·[/color] 像素[/b]
场景与 HUD 一起像素化。

[b][color=#B78A50]13 ·[/color] 描边[/b]
场景与 HUD 变为黑底白线，[b]不透视墙后物体[/b]。

[b][color=#B78A50]14 ·[/color] 大头[/b]
他人看见大头角色，持有者的场景与 HUD 呈现强烈鱼眼畸变。

[size=4][color=#B78A50][b]配置与说明[/b][/color][/size]

首次加载后编辑 [b]BepInEx/config/calypso.invokyr.expansionset.cfg[/b]，修改后重启。

[b]默认配置[/b]
[spoiler]
[b]NaturalSpawning = true[/b]
新雕像参与自然生成。

[b]DisableBoardPawnCollision = true[/b]
关闭棋盘进度棋子的碰撞，方便移动和踢骰子；显示、动画、棋盘、骰子及事件棋子保持正常。各端独立生效，联机建议一致。

[b]ShowIndividualIdolNames = false[/b]
开启显示各自中文名；关闭保留原版名称，MOD 使用统一名称，谏言照常显示。

[b]DebugMode = false[/b]
可选测试工具与日志；开启后按 F8 打开生成窗口（仅房主生成），正常玩法无需调试模式。
[/spoiler]

[i]游戏内名称与谏言目前为中文。[/i]

[i]当前为开发版本，完整联机与存读档兼容仍待验证；接力疲劳和怪相候选历史不入存档。[/i]
